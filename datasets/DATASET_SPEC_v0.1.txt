SETU NER Risk Dataset v0.1

Target geography:
Guwahati -> Imphal corridor

Primary objective:
Predict a disruption proxy for a road/network segment.

Primary target:
disruption_proxy

Data sources:
1. OpenStreetMap / Overpass
2. Open-Meteo historical weather
3. GDACS disaster/event data

Core features:
- segment_id
- timestamp
- latitude
- longitude
- elevation_m
- road_type
- segment_length_km
- precipitation_mm
- wind_speed_kmh
- wind_gust_kmh
- temperature_c
- humidity_pct
- weather_code
- nearby_disaster_count
- nearest_disaster_distance_km
- disaster_type
- disaster_severity
- accessibility_score
- vehicle_type
- disruption_proxy

Important:
disruption_proxy is a derived prototype label, not a claim of observed road closure.
Real observations and derived/synthetic data must remain distinguishable.

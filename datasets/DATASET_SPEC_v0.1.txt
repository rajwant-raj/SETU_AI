SETU NER Risk Dataset v0.1

Target geography:
Guwahati -> Imphal corridor

Primary objective:
Predict a disruption proxy for a road/network segment.

Primary target:
disruption_proxy

Data sources:
1. OpenStreetMap / Overpass
2. Open-Meteo historical weather
3. GDACS disaster/event data

Core features:
- segment_id
- timestamp
- latitude
- longitude
- elevation_m
- road_type
- segment_length_km
- precipitation_mm
- wind_speed_kmh
- wind_gust_kmh
- temperature_c
- humidity_pct
- weather_code
- nearby_disaster_count
- nearest_disaster_distance_km
- disaster_type
- disaster_severity
- accessibility_score
- vehicle_type
- disruption_proxy
- label_type
- label_source
- label_derivation_method

Label provenance contract:
- label_type is required and must be one of: observed, derived, synthetic.
- observed means the target label is directly recorded as a real-world operational observation from an identified source; label_source must identify that source and label_derivation_method must be "direct_observation".
- derived means the target label is computed from one or more real/public signals because direct ground-truth labels are unavailable; label_source must identify the input data sources and label_derivation_method must describe the deterministic derivation rule.
- synthetic means the target label is generated for testing, simulation, or augmentation rather than observed from a real event; label_source must identify the generator or simulation source and label_derivation_method must describe the generation rule.
- label_source and label_derivation_method must not be left ambiguous or represented as observed when the label is derived or synthetic.

Important:
disruption_proxy is a derived prototype label, not a claim of observed road closure.
Real observations, derived labels, and synthetic labels must remain distinguishable through the label provenance fields above.
